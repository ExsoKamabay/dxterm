A fix for installing Debian.

The Debian image host, easycli.sh, stopped serving the file, so installing
Debian failed in 1.0.0. The pinned SHA-256 refused those wrong bytes, which is
what it is for, but Debian could not be installed either way.

The Debian entry now points at images.linuxcontainers.org, which publishes its
own SHA256SUMS. The download is 90 MB and needs about 520 MB installed.

Kali Linux is unchanged, and so are the terminal and xset.
