First public release.

An ARM64 terminal emulator. Its ANSI/VT engine, PTY handling and UTF-8
rendering are written from scratch in C++.

- A BusyBox shell that works straight away, without root.
- Linux distros through PRoot: Debian 13 and three Kali builds, fetched only
  when you ask and checked against a pinned SHA-256.
- Up to five workspaces, sessions kept alive in the background.
- Type xset for themes, fonts and colours, with a live preview.
