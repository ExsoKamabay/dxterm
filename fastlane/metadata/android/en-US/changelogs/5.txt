The Linux session now runs on VHDP, a rootless engine inside the APK. PRoot
stays as an automatic fallback.

- The APK also runs on x86_64: emulators, WayDroid, ChromeOS.
- Fixed: tar -cf failing, SIGPIPE and SIGUSR1 never arriving, /dev missing
  from the guest on real phones.
- New: the vhdp command in the guest and a Diagnostics page in xset.
- The chosen language now survives a screen rotation.
- Distro images come from the project's own mirror.
